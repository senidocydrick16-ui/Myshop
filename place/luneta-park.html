Luneta Park

Luneta Park, also known as Rizal Park, is a historic urban park located in the heart of Manila. It is one of the largest urban parks in the Philippines and features the iconic monument of Jose Rizal.

Place Details

Address Ermita, Manila, Metro Manila
Opening Hours 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Entrance Fee Free
Best Time to Visit Late Afternoon
